Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 transformation has reshaped hiring. ## What a Saudi-Ready CV Looks Like

### Format essentials

- **1–2 pages**, single-column layout, standard fonts — no graphics-heavy designs that break ATS parsing.
- **Text-based PDF** (selectable text, not a scanned image), unless the job post asks for Word.
- **Standard section headings**: Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications.
- **Contact block**: phone with country code, professional email, city (e.g., Riyadh), LinkedIn URL. A full street address is unnecessary.
- **Photo**: generally not required and often better omitted; include only if the employer asks.

### Content essentials

- **A sharp 3–4 line summary**: who you are, your specialty, years of experience, and the value you bring.
- **Achievement bullets, not duty lists**: start with strong verbs, quantify results ("reduced onboarding time by 30%", "managed a SAR 2M budget").
- **Keywords from the job ad**, used honestly — ATS ranking is largely keyword matching.
- **Saudi-relevant signals** where true: experience with local regulations, projects in the region, Arabic proficiency level, familiarity with Vision 2030 sectors.
- **Certifications spelled out with acronyms**: "Project Management Professional (PMP)".

### What to leave out

Salary history, religion, marital status and other personal details (unless specifically requested), long paragraphs, and decorative skill meters that ATS software cannot read.

## English or Arabic? Usually: Both

The language question trips up almost everyone:

- **English CV**: multinationals, banks, consulting, tech, giga-projects, and virtually all LinkedIn applications. If the job ad is in English, apply in English.
- **Arabic CV**: government entities (Jadarat), many local companies, legal/administrative/education roles. If the ad is in Arabic, apply in Arabic.
- **Never a mixed-language single file**: bidirectional text (right-to-left Arabic plus left-to-right English) confuses ATS parsing and wastes space. Two clean, separate versions always win.
